Magnet Train Privacy Policy

Effective: November 7, 2026

BLCEAN ("we") handles information of people who play the Android game Magnet Train (package com.magnettrain.app, the "Game") as described below. The Game has no sign-up and never asks for or collects your name, email address or phone number.

1. Information stored on your device

  • Stage progress (cleared stages, stars, best move counts), settings (sound, vibration, etc.) and your chosen train style
  • Records of hints and cosmetic items earned, and how many ads you watched today
  • A random identifier for this installation (used to merge reward records across devices; not linked to you personally)

This information stays in the Game's storage on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game.

2. Information processed by third-party services

The Game uses the following Google services. Each processes information under the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

ServicePurposeInformation processed
Google Play Games ServicesSign-in and cloud save (continue on another device)Play Games player ID and nickname; the progress and reward records in section 1 (stored in Play Games saved games on your Google account)
Google AdMobRewarded ads, shown only when you choose (hints, item unlocks)Advertising ID, device and OS information, IP address (approximate location), ad impressions and clicks
Google Analytics for FirebaseUsage statistics to improve the Game (e.g. which stages are hard)App instance ID, device, OS and country, sessions, in-game actions (stage start, clear and quit, move and undo counts, hint and ad use, item equips)

Where consent is required (EEA, UK, Switzerland and similar), the Game shows Google's consent form (UMP) on first launch to ask about personalized ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings under Google > Ads.

3. How we use and share information

Information is used only for Game features (saving, continuing, rewards), serving ads, and improving the Game. We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone other than the service provider listed above (Google). All transfers are encrypted (HTTPS).

4. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data: uninstall the Game or use "Reset progress" in the Game's settings.
  • Cloud save: delete this Game's data from the Play Games app (or your Google account settings).
  • Usage statistics: kept in Google Analytics for up to 14 months, then deleted.
  • To ask for access or deletion, email us at the address below and we will help as far as the information we hold allows. Step by step: Data deletion

5. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
